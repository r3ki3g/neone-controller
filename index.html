One-time setup to auto-connect

This Chrome can't remember lights between visits yet, so you'd have to add them again after every refresh.

  1. Open this page from its https:// link, not from a downloaded or shared file.
  2. Open chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features in a new tab and set it to Enabled.
  3. Do the same for chrome://flags/#enable-web-bluetooth-new-permissions-backend.
  4. Press Relaunch, come back here and add each light once. After that they connect automatically.

Screen colour sync PRO

The lights follow the main colour on a computer screen, like an ambient TV light. On the computer, open this page and press Send this screen; on the phone with the lights, press Follow my screen. Both must be set to the same person. Copy-protected video (Netflix, Disney+) shows up black and can't be followed.

Paste the project's access key once on each device. Ending it with ---YourName also picks who you are.
